Receipt & invoice extractor

Paste the text of a receipt, invoice, or order confirmation, the kind that arrives in an email body. A server-side call to Claude reads it and returns clean, structured fields: no manual entry. Nothing you paste is stored.

This demo takes pasted text to keep it fast and free to try. The same extraction runs on uploaded PDFs and scanned documents in a production build.
